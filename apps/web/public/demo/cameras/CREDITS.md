the homepage walkthrough. They are not recordings of AniDachi users or endorsements.
The original local filenames (`john` and `jane`) are retained as asset identifiers.

no audio, fast-start metadata. JPEG posters use the frame at 0.3 seconds.
John: starts at 3.5 seconds, crop 566 x 566 at (566, 0).
Jane: starts at 0 seconds, crop 480 x 480 at (700, 70).
